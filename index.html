Homework · before class
🧽 Make Your Foam Stamp Prints

Do this before class.
The video shows how to make your stamp.

▶ Watch: make your foam stamp
  • Cut the foam to the size you want
  • Clean off any dust
  • Use a ballpoint pen
  • Press hard to make lines in the foam
Lines you draw

NO ink → they stay white

Flat parts

Ink → they print in colour

💡 Tips
  • Press hard so the lines are deep
  • Simple designs work best
  • Press the ink pad onto the foam
  • All the flat parts need ink
  • The lines you drew should have no ink
  • Put the foam face down on paper
  • Press evenly on the back
  • Lift up to see your print

Make at least 3 different prints. Try:

  • Different patterns
  • Different colours
  • Printing on top of each other (layering)

Task 1
💻 Edit Your Print in Photoshop

Clean your scanned print.
Remove the white paper. Keep only the print.

▶ Watch first, then follow the steps

File › Open → find your scanned print

  • Image › Adjustments › Levels
  • Move the white slider to the left
  • The grey paper turns white

First: is there a 🔒 on your layer? Click it to unlock.

Choose one way to select the white:

Option A · Rectangular Marquee

Choose the Rectangular Marquee Tool (M).
Drag a small box on a white area.

Option B · Magic Wand

Choose the Magic Wand Tool (W).
Click on a white area.

Then:

  1. Select › Similar — now all the white is selected
  2. Press Delete
  3. Select › Deselect Ctrl + D (Mac: Cmd + D)
Why?

Now only your print is left. You can put it on any background.

  • Use the Eraser Tool (E)
  • Remove dust, dirt or unwanted marks
  • Change colour: Image › Adjustments › Hue/Saturation
  • Copy the layer: Layer › Duplicate Layer Ctrl + J (Mac: Cmd + J)
  • Add a shadow: Layer › Layer Style › Drop Shadow
  • Keep transparency: File › Save As… → PSD or PNG
  • Export: File › Export › Export As… → JPG → print-after.jpg

Task 2
🖼️ Build a Research Page

Put your print on an A3 research page.
Add notes about your work.

File › Open → your A3 scan sheet. This is your background.

  1. Open your clean print from Task 1
  2. Select it all: Select › All Ctrl + A (Mac: Cmd + A)
  3. Copy: Edit › Copy Ctrl + C (Mac: Cmd + C)
  4. Go to your A3 sheet. Paste: Edit › Paste Ctrl + V (Mac: Cmd + V)
  5. Move and resize: Edit › Free Transform Ctrl + T (Mac: Cmd + T)
  • Click your print layer
  • In the Layers panel, find the menu that says Normal
  • Choose Multiply — white disappears
  • Try other modes too

Your page needs:

  • Subheadings — a title for each part
  • Annotation — notes about your process and ideas
  • References — artist names or image sources
  • Try different layouts
  • Add more prints or images
  • Play with colours and effects
  • Export: File › Export › Export As… → JPG → page.jpg
✅ Your research page includes
  • Your foam prints — edited and clean
  • Subheadings
  • Annotation — what you did and why
  • References

Research page examples

A3 sheets combined with prints
